# Third-party AI tools: billing, responsibility, and no reimbursement

**Status:** Project policy  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-09-22

YazSes welcomes contributions made with or without AI coding assistants. Tools such as
Claude Code, Codex, ChatGPT, Gemini, Cursor, Copilot, Jules, and similar services are
**optional third-party products**. They are not required to contribute.

This notice applies whenever a contributor chooses to use a third-party AI, cloud, hosted
development, model API, or paid automation service while working on YazSes.

## No purchase is required

You never need to buy an AI subscription, API credits, tokens, cloud compute, a usage
bundle, a rate-limit reset, an upgraded plan, or any other paid product in order to
contribute to YazSes.

A task described as "agent-ready", "cloud-ready", or suitable for a named tool means only
that the task can be completed with that workflow. It does **not** mean YazSes is asking
you to spend money.

If an agent or provider says that continuing requires a purchase, paid overage, credit
reload, plan upgrade, or paid cloud resource, you may stop. Waiting for an included quota
to reset or completing the task without that service is always acceptable.

## Your account and charges are your responsibility

Third-party services are provided, billed, and controlled by their own providers, not by
YazSes or its maintainers.

If you choose to use one, you are responsible for:

- choosing the account, subscription, model, API key, cloud provider, and billing mode;
- understanding the provider's current prices, quotas, credits, overage behavior, taxes,
  renewal terms, and cancellation rules;
- configuring any budget, spend limit, usage-credit, auto-reload, or overage controls;
- monitoring your own usage and invoices;
- ensuring that you are authorized to use the payment method, employer account, cloud
  account, API key, or subscription involved; and
- stopping the tool if its cost or billing behavior is not clear to you.

YazSes maintainers cannot see, configure, cap, reset, refund, or cancel usage on your
third-party account.

## No reimbursement or authority to spend

YazSes does not reimburse contributor expenses for AI subscriptions, API usage, tokens,
credits, cloud compute, network charges, or related services **unless a maintainer has
agreed in writing in advance to a specific expense and amount**.

A GitHub issue, label, task description, prompt example, review comment, discussion,
documentation page, or suggestion to use an AI tool is not authorization to incur a cost
on behalf of YazSes or a maintainer.

No contributor, agent, reviewer, or maintainer is authorized to create a financial
obligation for another maintainer or contributor merely by recommending a tool.

## Voluntary contribution; no employment or contractor relationship

Ordinary contributions to this public open-source repository are voluntary. Opening an
issue, taking a task, using an `agent-ready` label, submitting a pull request, receiving
review, or having a contribution merged does not by itself create employment, a contractor
relationship, agency, partnership, joint venture, a promise of compensation, or a right to
expense reimbursement.

If YazSes or a maintainer separately agrees in writing to paid work, reimbursement, a grant,
a bounty, or another compensated arrangement, that separate written agreement controls that
specific arrangement.

Nothing in this notice is intended to waive rights or obligations that applicable law does
not permit the parties to waive.

## No provider affiliation or endorsement

References to Claude, Anthropic, Codex, ChatGPT, OpenAI, Gemini, Google, Cursor, Copilot,
GitHub, Jules, or other third-party products are informational. Unless explicitly stated in
a separate written announcement, YazSes is not representing that a provider sponsors,
endorses, funds, warrants, or is affiliated with the project.

Third-party names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

## Provider documentation and terms control

The project may publish setup examples to help contributors avoid mistakes. Those examples
are informational and can become outdated because providers change products, plans, prices,
models, limits, and billing behavior.

Before enabling paid usage, check the provider's current official documentation, pricing,
billing settings, and terms. If a project example conflicts with the provider's current
documentation or the settings shown in your own account, **the provider's current terms and
your account settings control**.

The practical guide at [Using AI coding agents safely](docs/contribute/ai-agents.md) links
to the provider documentation that was current when it was last reviewed. It deliberately
does not copy fixed token prices into the repository.

## Credentials, data, and permissions

Do not put API keys, access tokens, passwords, payment data, private repository credentials,
or other secrets into an issue, pull request, commit, prompt, transcript, or project file.

Use the least access necessary. For normal contributions, work from your own fork and open
a pull request; you do not need write access to the upstream YazSes repository.

You are also responsible for deciding whether a provider's data-handling terms are suitable
for the code, logs, files, or other material you send to that provider. Do not upload
private, confidential, employer-restricted, security-sensitive, or personal data unless you
are authorized to do so.

**YazSes's offline-by-default product guarantee does not make a cloud coding agent offline.**
If you choose Claude, ChatGPT/Codex, Gemini, Jules, Copilot, or another hosted development
service, material you provide to that service may leave your machine under that provider's
terms. Never send real user recordings, transcripts, voiceprints, learning-corpus material,
research-participant data, or undeidentified tester logs to a third-party coding agent.
Use synthetic or explicitly de-identified fixtures instead.

## AI output is still the contributor's work to review

Using an agent does not transfer authorship or responsibility to the tool. The human who
opens the pull request must read the diff, understand the change, remove secrets or personal
data, and run the required validation.

YazSes may decline an AI-assisted contribution that is unverified, fabricated, outside the
task scope, or inconsistent with the project's privacy and safety rules.

## Limitation relating to third-party services

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, YazSes maintainers and contributors are
not responsible for charges, overages, lost credits, account restrictions, provider
outages, provider billing errors, provider data handling, or other loss arising from a
contributor's voluntary use of a third-party service.

Nothing in this notice creates a warranty, reimbursement promise, agency relationship,
partnership, or authorization to spend money on someone else's behalf.

If a separate written agreement with a maintainer expressly covers an expense, that
agreement controls that specific expense.
