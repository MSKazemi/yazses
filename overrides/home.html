Designed — and deliberately unbuilt

Cloud escalation is fully specified and stays unimplemented, so the offline promise can never erode by increments. Voiceprints, the learning corpus and third-party capture may never leave at all.

ADR-019 {{ ic("arrow-right") }}
{{ ic("puzzle-outline") }}

Declined, with reasons

Third-party plug-ins would sit on the hot path with your microphone, transcript and keyboard. They stay out until a real isolation boundary exists.

ADR-018 {{ ic("arrow-right") }}
{{ ic("tune-variant") }}

Gated on a measurement

Personal speech adapters — including for dysarthria, ALS and Parkinson’s — ship only after a measured accuracy win on held-out data. Ideas don’t ship on their own strength.
