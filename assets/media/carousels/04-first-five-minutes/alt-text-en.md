# 04-first-five-minutes — alt text (paste per slide in Instagram → Advanced settings → Accessibility)

01. Installed? Six commands get you from zero to dictating in about five minutes.
02. yazses quickstart is a read-only, three-step guide tailored to your machine. Real output excerpt.
03. yazses doctor checks your microphone, hotkey and typing backend.
04. yazses mic-level --set measures your room, then your voice, and sets the silence threshold between them.
05. yazses start, then yazses verify records, transcribes and names the exact link that breaks if something fails.
06. yazses autostart enable starts YazSes at login so it survives a reboot.
07. The whole setup, on one slide. Docs at mskazemi.com/yazses, code at github.com/MSKazemi/yazses. Free, Apache-2.0, offline. QR code (Troubleshooting): https://mskazemi.com/yazses/troubleshooting.html.
