# Your first 5 minutes - Instagram caption (English)

Installed it. Now what? Six commands. ⏱️

1. `yazses quickstart`: a read-only guide for your machine
2. `yazses doctor`: checks mic, hotkey and typing
3. `yazses mic-level --set`: tunes it to your voice and room
4. `yazses start`
5. `yazses verify`: proves the whole chain works
6. `yazses autostart enable`: there after every reboot

Each one tells you what to do next. Save this for setup day.
