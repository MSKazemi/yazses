# Meeting mode - Instagram caption (English)

Record the whole meeting. No bot joins the call. 👥

Turn it on once with `yazses features enable meeting`. Then `yazses meeting start` … `yazses meeting stop` gives you a transcript with who said what. Recording other people? Tell them first.

Speaker names come from voiceprints you enroll yourself, never from a cloud account. Minutes
(summary, decisions, action items) are optional, written by a local LLM on your own disk. The
recording is deleted after transcription unless you choose to keep it.

Made for meetings that shouldn't go to a note-taking SaaS.
