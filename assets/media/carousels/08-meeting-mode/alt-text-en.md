# 08-meeting-mode — alt text (paste per slide in Instagram → Advanced settings → Accessibility)

01. Meeting mode: record a whole meeting and get a who-said-what transcript. No bot joins the call.
02. Turn Meeting Mode on once, then yazses meeting start and yazses meeting stop. No key to hold. Tell people before you record them.
03. Speaker names come from voiceprints you enroll yourself from a kept recording, never from a cloud account.
04. Optional minutes: summary, decisions and action items written by a local LLM you supply.
05. The recording is deleted after transcription unless you ask to keep it. meeting summary says what not to trust.
06. Who it is for: confidential internal, clinical, legal or pre-publication research meetings.
07. Your meeting. Your machine. Docs at mskazemi.com/yazses, code at github.com/MSKazemi/yazses. Free, Apache-2.0, offline. QR code (Meeting guide): https://mskazemi.com/yazses/meeting-notes-offline.html.
