# What it is - Instagram caption (English)

Hold a key. Speak. It types. 🎙️

YazSes is free, open-source voice dictation that runs entirely on your own computer. Hold a key,
talk, let go, and your words appear in whatever app you are using: editor, browser, terminal, chat.

No cloud. No account. No API key. No subscription. Linux, macOS and Windows.

It also transcribes recordings you already have and can capture a whole meeting with speaker labels.
