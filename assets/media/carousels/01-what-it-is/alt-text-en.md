# 01-what-it-is — alt text (paste per slide in Instagram → Advanced settings → Accessibility)

01. YazSes: hold a key, speak, and your words are typed into any app. Offline, free, open source.
02. How it works: hold the hotkey, speak, release. Your words are typed at the cursor.
03. Nothing leaves your computer by default: no cloud, no account, no API key, no subscription.
04. Three things it does: dictate into any app, transcribe a recording, capture a whole meeting.
05. The default hotkey per OS: Right Alt on Linux, Right Option on macOS, Right Ctrl on Windows.
06. Speed: median 1.6 seconds from release to text with base.en, 0.9 seconds with tiny.en, on a modern laptop CPU.
07. Try it today. One install, then talk. Docs at mskazemi.com/yazses, code at github.com/MSKazemi/yazses. Free, Apache-2.0, offline. QR code (Docs home): https://mskazemi.com/yazses/.
