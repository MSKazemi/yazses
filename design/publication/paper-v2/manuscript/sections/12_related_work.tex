\section{Related Work}

Whisper established a widely used open-weight baseline for robust multilingual speech recognition
\citep{radford2022whisper}, while LibriSpeech remains a standard read-speech evaluation corpus
\citep{panayotov2015librispeech}. The present work is not intended to introduce a new recognizer;
it evaluates how several local recognizers and decoder configurations behave when integrated into
an interactive offline system.

Speaker diarization has a distinct literature and evaluation methodology from ASR. The AMI
Meeting Corpus provides a long-running benchmark for multi-party meeting research
\citep{carletta2005ami}. Our use of AMI is specifically to test whether a clustering operating
point that appeared reasonable in shorter or synthetic conditions transfers to long, annotated
meetings.

A complete related-work section must additionally cover four areas before submission:
repeated-decode non-determinism and fallback behavior in Whisper/faster-whisper-style systems;
previous-text conditioning and repetition loops; CPU streaming and partial-hypothesis stability;
and domain transfer in speaker-clustering thresholds. Repository issue \#507 tracks this current
literature verification. Until that search is complete, this manuscript deliberately avoids
priority claims such as first or previously unreported.
