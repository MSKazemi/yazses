# Communication templates — YazSes paper v2 authorship and final approval

These templates implement the three-gate protocol in README.md.

Replace bracketed fields before sending. Keep private information in private channels. Do not paste a contributor's private email address into a public GitHub issue or discussion.

## 1. Initial public GitHub contact

**Title:** Invitation to participate as an author of the second YazSes paper

Hello @GITHUB_HANDLE,

We are preparing the second YazSes research paper based on the project's work and the newer benchmark/evaluation campaign. The project plan is to invite **every contributor in the frozen YazSes contributor roster** to participate as an author, including contributors whose work was code, documentation, translation, testing, field reports, design review, research, or platform validation.

I would like to ask whether you want to be considered for the author list.

Nothing will be published with your name without your explicit agreement. If you opt in, you will be asked to review the manuscript and later approve the exact final version before publication. Under this paper's policy, publication will not proceed until every invited contributor has explicitly agreed and every confirmed author has approved the final frozen text.

Please reply with one of these:

- **YES — contact me privately for the author information form**
- **NO — I do not want to be an author**
- **QUESTION — I would like more information first**

Please do not post a private email address here unless you intentionally want it public.

Thank you again for your contribution to YazSes.

## 2. Private authorship invitation email

Hello [NAME],

Thank you for contributing to YazSes.

We are preparing a second research paper that covers the project's newer measurements and results. The project has chosen an inclusive authorship process: every contributor in the frozen contributor roster is invited to participate as an author.

Being invited does not automatically put your name on the paper. I need your explicit consent.

If you choose YES, you will be asked to:

1. confirm the exact name you want used in the publication;
2. provide your affiliation, or say “Independent” / no affiliation if that is your preference;
3. optionally provide an ORCID;
4. identify the contribution roles that describe your work;
5. review the draft and raise any concerns or corrections;
6. approve the exact final manuscript before publication.

Your decision has no effect on your existing project credit, your software contribution, or your ability to contribute to YazSes in the future.

Please reply with one of the following:

**YES — I consent to participate in the YazSes paper v2 authorship process.**

or

**NO — I do not consent to be an author of this paper.**

or

**QUESTION — I need clarification before deciding: [question]**

If you answer YES, please also complete the author information form below.
